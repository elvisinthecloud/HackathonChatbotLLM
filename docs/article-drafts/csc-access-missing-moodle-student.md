## Knowledge content — draft, do not ingest

To access CSC course content:

1. Sign in to **MCeLE** and confirm that CSC appears under **My Courses**.
2. Open Moodle by selecting the **Instructor-Led Courses (Moodle)** circle on the Portal page, or select **My Learning** and then **Instructor-Led Courses**.
3. In Moodle, open **My Courses** and set the drop-down to **All** so every Moodle course is displayed.
4. Open the CSC course when it appears.

If CSC appears in MCeLE/My Courses but is missing after Moodle opens, contact **[AUTHORING DECISION REQUIRED: Instructor first / Region first / Instructor followed by Region]** so the Moodle-side enrollment can be checked and any required enrollment action can be coordinated.

If CSC does not appear under MCeLE **My Courses** at all, contact the **Help Desk first** so the account, course visibility, and apparent enrollment can be checked. If that review confirms that an enrollment action is required, contact the appropriate **Region**.
