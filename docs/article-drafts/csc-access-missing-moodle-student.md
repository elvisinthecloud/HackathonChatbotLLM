## Knowledge content

CSC course content is delivered in Moodle. Course discovery and enrollment are handled in MCeLE.

### Access your CSC course

1. Sign in to MCeLE and check whether CSC appears under **My Courses**.
2. If CSC is listed, open Moodle by selecting the **Instructor-Led Courses (Moodle)** circle on the Portal page, or select **My Learning** and then **Instructor-Led Courses**.
3. In Moodle, open **My Courses** and set the drop-down to **All** to display all your Moodle courses.
4. Open CSC when it appears.

### CSC appears in MCeLE but is missing in Moodle

If CSC appears under MCeLE **My Courses** but is still missing from Moodle **My Courses** with **All** selected, contact the appropriate **Region first** to have the Moodle-side enrollment checked and any required enrollment action coordinated.

### CSC is missing from MCeLE My Courses

If CSC does not appear under MCeLE **My Courses** at all, contact the **Help Desk first** to check your account, course visibility, and apparent enrollment.

If that review confirms that an enrollment action is required, contact the appropriate **Region** to handle that action.
