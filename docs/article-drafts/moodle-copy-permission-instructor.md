## Knowledge content

Copying or creating courses in Moodle requires the **Academics Officer (AO) Moodle role**. Your selected **Instructor (Adjunct Faculty)** profile does not have that role.

You will need to obtain the appropriate AO permissions or contact a user with the AO role to copy the course for you.
