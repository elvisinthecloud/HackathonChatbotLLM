# EPME course eligibility and enrollment requirements

## Article record

- **Article ID:** `MCELE-EPME-001`
- **Service/area:** MCeLE
- **Allowed role:** Student
- **Course scope:** EPME3000, EPME4000, EPME5000, EPME6000, EPME5500, and EPME6800
- **Content type:** Adapted, source-supported guidance

## Knowledge content

Use these requirements to determine EPME eligibility:

- **EPME3000 / EPME3000AA:** All Marines.
- **EPME4000 / EPME4000AA:** Lance Corporals and above; must complete EPME3000.
- **EPME5000 / EPME5000BA:** Sergeants and above. **Sergeant selects cannot enroll; the Marine must hold the rank of Sergeant or above.**
- **EPME6000 / EPME6000BA:** Staff Sergeant selects and above.
- **EPME5500:** Sergeants; must complete EPME5000BA.
- **EPME6800:** Staff Sergeants and Gunnery Sergeants; must complete EPME6000.

For **EPME5500** and **EPME6800**, request enrollment in MCeLE, attach a command-endorsed **NAVMC 11580**, and meet the applicable physical/body-composition requirements.

If the Marine cannot enroll or recently received a new rank or select grade, contact the **MCeLE Help Desk** to verify the rank or select status reflected in MCTFS and receive enrollment assistance.
