# Can I repeat a completed course for additional Reserve Retirement Credits?

## Article record

- **Article ID:** `MCELE-RRC-001`
- **Service/area:** MCeLE
- **Allowed role:** Student
- **Course scope:** General Reserve Retirement Credit guidance
- **Content type:** Adapted, source-supported guidance

## Knowledge content

A course that you have already completed cannot be completed again to receive **Reserve Retirement Credits (RRC)** a second time. Beginning a new **calendar year**, **fiscal year**, or **anniversary year** does not make the same completed course eligible for another RRC award.

Review the MCeLE catalog for other courses that award RRC:

1. Open the **Course Catalog**.
2. Use the **Item Has** filter to find courses that provide RRC.
3. Review a course's search-result tile or open its **Full Details** tab to see the number of Reserve Retirement Credits awarded upon completion.
4. Select a different eligible course to earn additional RRC.

This guidance does not create calendar-year, fiscal-year, or anniversary-year exceptions.
